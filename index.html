Before you choose.

Does one purchase include every layout?

Yes. All twelve layouts in v1.0.1 are included. They are delivered as four source projects; the two concept projects each contain five layout routes.

Can I change the products and branding?

Yes. Edit catalog data, colors, content and images in the supplied source projects. A Vue/Nuxt developer can adapt the template to your business.

Does this include a working payment system?

The previews demonstrate storefront interactions using mock state. They do not process payments, create real accounts or submit orders. Backend services are not included.

Is this an English-only template?

Yes. All twelve layouts, supporting pages, shopping messages and sample content are in English. A multilingual translation system is not included.

Are lifetime updates included?

The download contains the current v1.0.1 release. Future updates and support depend on the seller's published terms.
